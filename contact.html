Data Privacy Standards

TECHNICAL RECRUITMENT DATA PRIVACY DISCLOSURE.

In a real recruitment business handling developer CVs, GitHub repositories, and confidential enterprise requisitions, strict data protection standards are legally and ethically essential.

  • No Storage in Demo: This TSCG Technologies demonstration project does not collect, transmit, store, or serialize candidate resumes or employer messages on any backend server or database.
  • Retention & Deletion Rights: Production recruitment firms must provide clear retention periods (e.g., 6–12 months with opt-in refresh) and a straightforward mechanism for candidates to request permanent record deletion at any time.
  • Consent-Driven Sharing: Candidate resumes should never be circulated to hiring companies without prior explicit authorization from the candidate regarding the exact role and employer.
