Engagement Models

RECRUITMENT DESIGNED FOR YOUR VELOCITY.

Flexible engagement models tailored to immediate contract spikes, permanent core squads, or executive leadership.

Model 01

IT Permanent Recruitment

Full-lifecycle talent acquisition for long-term engineering personnel who shape your core product architecture, culture, and IP.

  • Thorough technical pre-screening on concurrency and system design.
  • Cultural calibration and compensation expectations management.
  • High retention focus with structured onboarding check-ins.
Model 02

Contract Technology Staffing

Agile, project-based engineering augmentation for critical milestones, migration sprints, or fixed-duration development initiatives.

  • Rapid deployment of pre-vetted contractors within days.

  • Turnkey contractor compliance and payroll administration coordination.
Model 03

Executive Technology Search

Confidential talent discovery for senior engineering leadership, including VP of Engineering, Chief Technology Officer, and Principal Architects.

  • Strategic evaluation of organizational scaling accomplishments.

  • Equity compensation alignment and governance advisory.

Bulk & Sprint Hiring

Coordinated recruitment drives for newly funded product pods, Global Capability Centers (GCCs), or regional expansion hubs.

  • Dedicated talent delivery pods for high-volume technical screening.

  • Real-time candidate pipeline dashboards and metric reporting.

Startup Scaling Support

Targeted support for early-stage and high-growth ventures hiring their founding engineers or first DevOps lead.

Rigorous Methodology

TECHNICAL CANDIDATE SCREENING PROCESS.

We eliminate non-technical applicant noise so your engineering managers can concentrate on high-signal final evaluations.

1. Hands-on Code & Syntax Verification:

Validating current versions (e.g. Python 3.12+, React 19, Java 21) and asynchronous patterns.

2. Production Architecture Dialogue:

Probing how candidates handled real outages, latency bottlenecks, and database locking in past roles.

3. Engineering Hygiene & Collaboration:

Assessing test automation discipline, documentation standards, and cross-functional communication.
